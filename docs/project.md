**Computational History Project: Writing the History of Himalayan Villages**

**Groups:** 3 students  
**Final submission:** 3,000–4,000 word essay, 4 Nov 2026  
**Presentations:** To be scheduled subsequently (ideally after end sems)

**The Project**

Using the 1961 Census Village Survey corpus, write a history of one or more Himalayan villages using the approaches to history we have encountered in the course, particularly Marxist and/or Annales historiography. The historical question, the theoretical perspective and the computational methods should emerge from your engagement with the sources, I will not be prescribing them in advance. Please refer to programminghistorian.org for possible ideas, methods and tools.

**Sources**

- **1961 Census Village Surveys:** detailed studies of individual villages covering their physical setting, population, social organisation, economy and cultural life.

- **1881 Census:** an earlier colonial enumeration providing broader information on the populations, social categories and occupations of the region.

- **1931 Census:** a later colonial enumeration allowing comparison with the categories and patterns recorded in 1881 and 1961.

- **H. A. Rose, *A Glossary of the Tribes and Castes of the Punjab and North-West Frontier Province* (1910):** a colonial ethnographic account of the communities and social categories of the region.

You may work with **one village, a selection of villages, or the entire corpus**.

**Three Stages**

Each of these will be documented: what you have done and by when.

1\. Research Question  
Identify the historical problem you want to investigate and explain your approach.

2\. Analysis  
Show how you have worked with the sources and what will be your computational analysis.

3\. Historical Interpretation  
Develop your findings into a historical argument drawing on Annales and / or Marxist methods and deploying computational tools for your analysis.
