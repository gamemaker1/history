Who takes a share of whose product: flows of surplus in each conjuncture

Arrows run from the party who gives up product, labour or money to the party who receives it. Line width is the number of sourced claims. Each actor sits in the same place in every panel, so relations that appear or vanish between periods can be compared by eye. Node size is the number of claims flowing in.
