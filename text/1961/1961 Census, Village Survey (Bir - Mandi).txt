selection for socio-economic survey was
made keeping in view the general pattern of

and the post Independence Changes are
the main factors which have some significant bearing on the general conditions of
life of the villagers. Some of the tillNEI of
land in Bir were only tenants as the land
belonged to the business community of

Abolition of big landed estates and Land
Reforms Act 1955 the tenants got the opportunit~ to acquire the proprietory rights after
paymg a nominal amount as compensation.

other castes living in the village will reveal
certain aspects of life which give an idea
how they live and behave with each other.
In this publication we have tried to give an
idea about the interdependence of castes. People have the opportunity of availaing of the
facilities slUch as better education for their

their homesi.
