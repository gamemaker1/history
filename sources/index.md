# Sources

This file lists every source the project has used or found. Section 1 lists
the files in `sources/`. Section 2 lists online copies and further sources.
Section 3 lists older texts that the 1961 surveys quote or reprint. Section 4
lists secondary literature named in the idea files in `ideas/`.

Links were checked on 27 September 2026. Census catalogue links were checked
with `curl`, because the site's certificate fails in some tools. Every
catalogue page returned HTTP 200, and every PDF link downloaded in full.

`text/` holds the searchable text of every offline source (see Section 1.5).

---

### 1.2 1881 Census

File: `Punjab 1881, Denzil Ibbetson-20260926T175319Z-1-001.zip` (67 MB).

Denzil Ibbetson, *Report on the Census of the Panjab, 1881*. Six PDFs:

1. Preface (19 pages)
2. Results, pp. 1 to 421 (421 pages). This part holds almost all the
   material used.
3. Operations (72 pages)
4. Supplementary Tables, Appendix C (31 pages)
5. Instructions Issued, Appendix D (26 pages)
6. Rough Vernacular Index (6 pages)

The zip also holds a `desktop.ini`, which is not a source.

## 4. Secondary literature named in the idea files in `ideas/`

These works are named in `ideas/candidate-topics.md` and the later idea files. None is in
`sources/`, and none has been checked.

**Region and topic**

- Chetan Singh, *Natural Premises: Ecology and Peasant Life in the Western
  Himalaya, 1800 to 1950*.
- Chetan Singh, "Constructing the State in the Western Himalaya," *Journal of
  Punjab Studies*, 2013.
- William S. Sax, *In the Valley of the Kauravas: A Divine Kingdom in the
  Western Himalaya* (Oxford University Press, 2024).
- Jonathan P. Parry, *Caste and Kinship in Kangra* (1979).
- Mahesh Sharma, "State Formation and Cultural Complex in Western Himalaya:
  Chamba Genealogy and Epigraphs, 700 to 1650 CE," *IESHR*, 2004.
- Pamela Kanwar, *Imperial Simla*.
- Dane Kennedy, *The Magic Mountains*.
- Judith Kenny, "Climate, Race, and Imperial Authority," *Annals of the
  Association of American Geographers*, 1995.
- Tan Tai Yong, *The Garrison State: The Military, Government and Society in
  Colonial Punjab, 1849 to 1947*.
- Malcolm Lyall Darling, *The Punjab Peasant in Prosperity and Debt* (1925).
- Richard P. Tucker, *A Forest History of India*.
- Ramachandra Guha, *The Unquiet Woods*.
- "The commodity exchange between Western Tibet and Kinnaur," *GeoJournal*.
- "Indo-Tibetan Wool Politics," *Himalayan Journal*.

**Theory**

- Fernand Braudel, *The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age
  of Philip II*. The Annales model of geographical, social and event time.
- Marc Bloch, *The Royal Touch*. History of mentalities.
- Daniel Thorner and Utsa Patnaik. Marxist agrarian history of India.
